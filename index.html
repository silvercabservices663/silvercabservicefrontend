Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking a Silver Cab in Sydney, airport transfers, vehicle options, and payment methods.

How do I book a Silver Cab in Sydney?

You can book directly by calling +61 410 854 028, sending an SMS text, or submitting our online inquiry form. Your chauffeur confirms your booking with a direct call.

Do you provide Sydney Airport taxi transfers?

Yes, we provide 24/7 Sydney Airport (SYD) transfer services across domestic (T2/T3) and international (T1) terminals with flight tracking and punctual arrival.

What vehicles are available in the Silver Cab fleet?

Our fleet features 4-seater Sedans (Toyota Camry), 4-seater Wagons (for extra luggage capacity), 7-seater Maxi Taxis, and 11-seater Maxi Taxis. Child safety baby seats are available upon request.

What payment methods do you accept for Silver Taxi Sydney rides?

We accept all major Credit/Debit Cards, Cabcharge, and Cash payments directly with your chauffeur.
